# SpiralQuant — Announcement (EN)

**🌀 SpiralQuant — a synthesizer that solves the Schrödinger equation in real time.**

Every voice is a genuine 128-point complex wavefunction ψ(x), evolved live by
split-step Fourier. You don't draw waveforms — you build the quantum box the wave
lives in: pick a particle shape, choose a potential (harmonic well, barrier,
double well…), and hear the wave slosh, reflect, interfere with itself and
*tunnel through walls*.

🎛 **What you get**
- Real-time quantum evolution at audio rates — the interference *is* the timbre
- 6 wave shapes × 6 potentials × 4 readout modes (Re/Im/|ψ|/phase) + momentum-FFT audio
- Quantum tunnelling (Gamow transparency) for evolving shimmering tails
- Just-intonation chord mode, ±48 semitones of transposition (±4 octaves), ADSR, filter, drive, 4 master FX
- Live scope of |ψ(x)| **and** |ψ̃(k)| spectrum fed by the actual DSP — never a fake animation
- 3 skins (Dark / SciFi Neon / Console), scalable UI, preset browser
- VST3 + AU + Standalone, macOS universal / Windows / Linux
- 8 voices ≈ 0.06× realtime CPU

Free & open source. Try: *InfiniteWell + narrow Gaussian packet + IMPULSE + TUNNEL*
— and listen to a wavefunction diffract.
